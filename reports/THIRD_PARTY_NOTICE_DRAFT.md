# Third-party notice draft

`NOTICE_FINAL=false`

This is a review draft, not an approved notice and not legal advice.

## Potential notices if corresponding assets are included

- OpenStreetMap: `© OpenStreetMap contributors`; database use is subject to
  ODbL 1.0. Exact produced-database and attribution placement requires human
  review.
- Project PLATEAU: identify the exact city/version/asset URL and reproduce the
  attribution required by that asset's official terms. Catalogue metadata alone
  does not authorize a blanket notice.
- Kyoto/Fujisawa official hazard data: name the exact publisher, dataset,
  revision, source URL, access date, and terms URL. A municipality label alone
  is insufficient.
- Hokonavi: the adapter prototype references the Ministry of Land,
  Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism 2024 walking-space network
  specification. It is not certification or approval, and no official network
  dataset is bundled by this statement.
- Python/Node dependencies: preserve licenses and bundled notices required by
  the exact lockfiles and distributed form. A source-only archive does not
  automatically carry the same obligations as redistributed binaries.

## Explicit exclusions

Copyrighted research PDFs, full PBF/CityGML/raster/ZIP inputs, license-unknown
files, secrets, absolute local paths, and Kyoto road-ledger plan content must not
enter a public archive.

The final notice must be regenerated from the exact release manifest after the
root license and every data redistribution decision are approved.
